    Physical health benifits


    • Builds strong bones and muscles. Weight-bearing activities like running, jumping, and climbing stimulate bone density and muscle growth during critical developmental years.

    • Strengthens the heart and lungs. Daily aerobic movement improves cardiovascular endurance, helps maintain healthy blood pressure, and lowers the risk of heart disease later in life.

    • Supports a healthy weight. Regular activity balances energy intake and reduces the risk of childhood obesity and metabolic conditions like type 2 diabetes.

    • Improves motor skills. Active play sharpens balance, agility, and hand-eye coordination.


    Brain & Academic Benefits


    • Boosts focus and memory. Physical activity increases blood flow to the brain, improving attention span, problem-solving skills, and classroom concentration.

    • Enhances academic performance. Kids who are consistently active tend to retain information better and perform stronger on school assignments and tests.
